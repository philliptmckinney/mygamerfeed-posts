Monster Hunter Wilds on Switch 2: 4 things to do before you play

It hits Switch 2 on December 4. Here is what to set up first, and what to expect from how it runs.

Updated November 26, 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answerClear about 40 GB of space, pick Prioritize Frame Rate if you play docked, and expect 30fps with no ray tracing in every mode. It has full cross-play with PlayStation, Xbox and PC.

Do these four

  1. Clear about 40 GBNintendo lists the estimated file size at 39 GB. Make room now so you're not stuck waiting on launch night.
  2. Docked? Pick Prioritize Frame RateThere are two TV modes. Prioritize Resolution outputs 2240 x 1260 and Prioritize Frame Rate outputs 1920 x 1080. Both target 30fps, and Capcom notes the frame rate can drop in heavy fights, so the frame rate mode gives you the most breathing room.
  3. Skip the 60fps and ray tracing huntNeither is on Switch 2. Every mode targets 30fps, and ray tracing is off in all of them. Handheld runs at 1536 x 864.
  4. Hunt with friends on any platformIt has full cross-play, so friends on PlayStation, Xbox and PC can join you without anyone starting over. Up to four can also play together on local wireless.
Haven't bought it yet? Capcom's digital launch price on Switch 2 is $29.99 through December 19.

Questions

Does Monster Hunter Wilds run at 60fps on Switch 2?

No. Capcom lists a 30fps target in every Switch 2 mode, docked and handheld.

How big is Monster Hunter Wilds on Switch 2?

Nintendo lists the estimated file size at about 39 GB.

Does the Switch 2 version have cross-play?

Yes. Capcom says it has full cross-play online, plus local wireless play with up to four hunters.

Does it include the free title updates?

Yes. Capcom says the Switch 2 version includes all free title updates. The Ascendance expansion is coming to Switch 2 in 2027.
